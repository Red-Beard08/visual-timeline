# Visual Timeline

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Obsidian users exploring history through local Markdown notes, initially church and biblical history.

## Product Purpose

Make continuity, succession, parallel histories, and explicit relationships understandable before drilling into individual records.

## Operating Context

An Obsidian add-on for desktop and mobile. The chart is a local, searchable and filterable display of notes, not a remote historical database.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Notes remain the canonical records. Preserve their content and explicit relationships.
- Support BC/AD, precise ISO dates, circa ancient dates, configurable rows and layers, saved compositions, and streams, bars, lines, dots, and bands.
- Zoom and pan change the time view, not historical associations.
- Selection reveals full details; opening a source note is a separate action.
- All assets and runtime behavior must remain local, with no ongoing API requirement.

## Brand Commitments

The name is Visual Timeline, within the Red-Beard Obsidian suite. Preserve the established flowing-stream direction, inspired by Adams/Ussher history charts, rather than reverting to disconnected blocks.

## Evidence on Hand

The existing implementation and a 48-record Israel example support design evaluation. This example is not a substitute for a general-purpose schema. Design mockups are illustrative, not historical research or evidence of implemented behavior.

## Product Principles

- Historical continuity first, individual detail second.
- Explicit references, never relationships inferred from names.
- Every record remains reachable even when its full name cannot fit.
- Preserve user configuration and notes.
